WeChat Pay

  1. WeChat → Me → Services → Wallet
  2. Tap Bills (top-right)
  3. Tap FAQ → Download Bill
  4. Choose For Personal Reconciliation, pick dates
  5. Enter email → download the zip → unzip it
  6. Upload the .csv / .xlsx inside below

Alipay

  1. Alipay → My → Settings → Bills
  2. Tap ··· → Get Transaction Statement
  3. Choose For Personal Reconciliation, pick dates
  4. Tick Show Counterparty Info and Show Product Description
  5. Enter email → download the file
  6. Upload the .csv / .xlsx below
